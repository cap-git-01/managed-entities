02 / The recurring problem

We have provider data. Not one Provider.

Multiple systems describe overlapping aspects of a provider. Important business concepts may not exist in any single source.

▤MD-StaffCredentialing, affiliations, status
▤EpicClinical, operational, scheduling context
▤Other systemsContracts, enrollment, claims, directories
Same provider?Same attribute?Same meaning?Who can change it?
Repeated across projects: identity resolution, competing definitions, source reconciliation, one-off integration logic, and meetings to decide what a field actually means.
Integration moves data. It does not, by itself, establish shared business meaning.

AI improves the economics—not the need.

The need for a governed business foundation predates AI. AI can lower engineering effort while agents increase the return on reuse.

↺

⌘

Return on reuseEach new project, workflow and agent consumes an existing governed entity and its operations

The investment case is measurable; a multimillion-dollar savings estimate should be validated bottom-up, not assumed.
